# Archery Performance Lab Manifesto

> *"Measure honestly. Understand deeply. Improve continuously."*

---

# Why APL Exists

Archery is a sport of extraordinary precision.

Every shot is the result of countless interactions between the athlete, the equipment and the environment.

Despite this complexity, much of today's technical decision-making still relies on personal experience, subjective perception and fragmented information.

Archery Performance Lab (APL) exists to change that.

APL is an open research platform dedicated to transforming archery performance analysis into a transparent, reproducible and evidence-based process.

Its purpose is not to replace coaches.

Its purpose is not to replace athletes.

Its purpose is to provide better information so that better decisions can be made.

---

# Our Vision

We believe that every archer, regardless of experience or competitive level, should have access to professional-quality analytical tools.

Knowledge should not depend on geography, budget or exclusive access to technology.

Scientific methodology should be available to everyone.

APL exists to democratize technical knowledge in archery.

---

# Our Mission

Our mission is to build the most transparent, modular and scientifically reliable open platform for archery performance analysis.

APL combines:

- engineering;
- biomechanics;
- data analysis;
- artificial intelligence;
- software engineering;
- coaching knowledge.

All working together to improve understanding of performance.

---

# What We Believe

We believe that:

Data without interpretation is incomplete.

Interpretation without evidence is unreliable.

Recommendations without explanation are unacceptable.

Technology should assist human judgment, never replace it.

Scientific honesty is more valuable than attractive conclusions.

Every analysis should be understandable.

Every conclusion should be verifiable.

Every improvement should be reproducible.

---

# Our Principles

APL is built upon eight architectural principles:

1. Domain Separation
2. Centralized Orchestration
3. Raw Data Preservation
4. Unified Product Classification
5. Explainable Intelligence
6. Domain Ownership
7. Historical Immutability
8. Unified Identification

These principles define the permanent foundation of the project.

Technology may evolve.

The principles remain.

---

# Open Research

APL is an open research initiative.

We believe that scientific progress accelerates when knowledge is shared.

Whenever possible, methodologies, documentation and technical decisions should remain publicly accessible.

Community contributions are welcome.

Constructive criticism is encouraged.

Evidence is preferred over opinion.

---

# Explainability Before Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is a powerful tool.

But intelligence without explainability cannot be trusted.

APL adopts Explainable Intelligence as a fundamental design principle.

Every recommendation shall be supported by:

- measured data;
- technical knowledge;
- transparent reasoning.

The user must always understand *why* a conclusion was reached.

---

# Respect for the Athlete

The athlete always comes first.

APL does not make decisions.

APL provides information.

The final decision belongs to the athlete and the coach.

Technology supports people.

People do not serve technology.

---

# Engineering Over Assumptions

Whenever possible:

Measure.

Validate.

Document.

Repeat.

APL favors measurable evidence over intuition.

Hypotheses are welcome.

Assertions require validation.

---

# Long-Term Thinking

APL is not designed for a single season.

Nor for a single athlete.

Nor for a single software release.

It is designed as a platform capable of evolving for decades while preserving scientific consistency.

Every architectural decision should consider the future.

---

# Community

APL belongs to its community.

Researchers.

Athletes.

Coaches.

Engineers.

Developers.

Contributors.

Everyone is invited to participate with respect, curiosity and intellectual honesty.

---

# Our Commitment

We commit to building software that is:

- transparent;
- documented;
- reproducible;
- modular;
- explainable;
- maintainable;
- open whenever possible.

We accept that mistakes will occur.

We commit to correcting them openly and continuously improving the project.

---

# The Future

APL is more than software.

It is a research platform.

A knowledge platform.

A collaboration platform.

A place where engineering and sport meet.

Its success will not be measured only by features or downloads.

It will be measured by the quality of the knowledge it helps create.
