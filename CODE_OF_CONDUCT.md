# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of the Archery Performance Lab (APL) project, pledge to make participation in our community a respectful, welcoming and constructive experience for everyone.

Participation in APL should be based on collaboration, scientific integrity and mutual respect, regardless of age, experience, background, nationality, gender, identity, disability, ethnicity, religion or any other personal characteristic.

---

# Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and professional.
- Welcoming constructive discussion.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Supporting scientific rigor and transparency.
- Sharing knowledge openly.
- Respecting different technical and scientific viewpoints.
- Focusing discussions on ideas rather than individuals.
- Helping newcomers learn and contribute.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or insults.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Abusive or offensive language.
- Deliberately spreading misinformation.
- Any conduct that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.

---

# Scientific Integrity

APL is founded on evidence-based research.

Contributors are expected to:

- Clearly distinguish observations from interpretations.
- Document assumptions.
- Preserve original data whenever possible.
- Avoid presenting speculation as established fact.
- Be transparent about limitations and uncertainties.

Scientific disagreement is welcome when expressed respectfully and supported by evidence.

---

# Community Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Promoting a respectful environment.
- Reviewing contributions fairly.
- Applying this Code of Conduct consistently.
- Taking appropriate action when necessary.

---

# Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainers.

Reports will be handled confidentially whenever reasonably possible.

---

# Enforcement

Project maintainers may:

- Request clarification.
- Issue warnings.
- Reject contributions.
- Remove inappropriate content.
- Temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Actions will be proportional to the severity and frequency of the behavior.

---

# Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues.
- Pull Requests.
- Discussions.
- Project documentation.
- Community spaces managed by APL.

---
