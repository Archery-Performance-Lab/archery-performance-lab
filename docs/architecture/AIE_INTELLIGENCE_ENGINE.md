# 1. Purpose

The APL Intelligence Engine (AIE) is the analytical intelligence layer of Archery Performance Lab.

AIE transforms validated data into technical insights, recommendations and decision support.

AIE does not generate or modify operational data.

Every analytical result shall be explainable, reproducible and traceable.

---

# 2. Responsibilities

AIE is responsible for:

- Technical Analysis
- Statistical Analysis
- Performance Evaluation
- Equipment Optimization
- Arrow Optimization
- Trend Analysis
- Pattern Recognition
- Decision Support

AIE never owns business data.

# 13. Three-Level Analytical Model

Every analytical response generated by AIE shall follow the same structure.

## Observation

Describes what has been objectively measured.

Examples:

- Group diameter increased.
- Average score decreased.
- Arrow speed remained stable.

Observations are based exclusively on Raw Data stored in APD.

---

## Interpretation

Explains the technical meaning of the observation.

Interpretations are generated using:

- AKB
- AKG

Interpretations shall never contradict measured data.

---

## Recommendation

Suggests possible actions.

Examples:

- Verify brace height.
- Check button preload.
- Repeat bare shaft tuning.
- Increase training volume.

Recommendations are advisory.

The final decision always belongs to the athlete or coach.

---

# 14. Analytical Workflow

Every analysis follows the same sequence.

```text
Raw Data (APD)

↓

Validation (ACE)

↓

Knowledge (AKB)

↓

Relationships (AKG)

↓

Analysis (AIE)

↓

Observation

↓

Interpretation

↓

Recommendation

↓

Validated Response
```

No analytical step may bypass this workflow.
