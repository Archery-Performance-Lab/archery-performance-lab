# ADR-005 – Explainable Analytical Model

**Project:** Archery Performance Lab (APL)

**ADR ID:** ADR-005

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-29

**Decision Makers:** Archery Performance Lab Team

---

# Context

The APL Intelligence Engine (AIE) generates technical analyses based on measured data, engineering knowledge and semantic relationships.

Traditional AI systems often return recommendations without clearly distinguishing:

- measured facts;
- technical interpretation;
- suggested actions.

This makes it difficult for coaches and athletes to understand the origin of a conclusion and to evaluate its reliability.

APL is intended to be a transparent, evidence-based platform.

Therefore every analytical response must clearly separate objective observations from interpretation and recommendations.

---

# Decision

APL adopts a mandatory three-level analytical model.

Every analytical response generated by AIE shall contain the following sections:

1. Observation
2. Interpretation
3. Recommendation

The three sections shall remain logically independent.

---

# Analytical Model

```text
Measured Data
        │
        ▼
 Observation
        │
        ▼
 Interpretation
        │
        ▼
 Recommendation
```

Each level builds upon the previous one.

No level may bypass another.

---

# Observation

Observation contains only objective facts.

Observations are generated exclusively from validated Raw Data.

Examples:

- Average score increased.
- Group diameter decreased.
- Wind speed changed.
- Average shot execution time increased.

Observation shall never contain opinions or recommendations.

---

# Interpretation

Interpretation explains the technical meaning of the observations.

Interpretations are produced using:

- AKB (Knowledge Base)
- AKG (Knowledge Graph)

Examples:

- Increased dispersion suggests reduced shooting consistency.
- Lower average score correlates with increased wind variability.
- Arrow grouping indicates a possible tuning issue.

Interpretation shall always be supported by technical knowledge.

---

# Recommendation

Recommendation proposes possible actions.

Examples:

- Verify brace height.
- Repeat bare shaft tuning.
- Check button preload.
- Review shot timing.
- Repeat the session under similar conditions.

Recommendations are advisory only.

The final decision always belongs to the athlete or coach.

---

# Explainability

Every recommendation shall include:

- Supporting Observation(s)
- Applied Technical Rule(s)
- Knowledge Source(s)
- Confidence Level

APL shall never produce unexplained recommendations.

---

# Traceability

Every analytical response shall be traceable to:

- Raw Data (APD)
- Technical Knowledge (AKB)
- Relationships (AKG)
- Analytical Model Version

No conclusion shall exist without supporting evidence.

---

# Responsibilities

## APD

Provides measured data.

## AKB

Provides technical knowledge.

## AKG

Provides semantic relationships.

## AIE

Produces the analytical response.

## ACE

Validates inputs and outputs.

---

# Architecture

```text
Raw Data (APD)

        │
        ▼

Observation

        │
        ▼

Knowledge (AKB)

+

Relationships (AKG)

        │
        ▼

Interpretation

        │
        ▼

Recommendation

        │
        ▼

Validation (ACE)

        │
        ▼

User
```

---

# Benefits

This model provides:

- Explainable AI
- Scientific transparency
- Traceability
- Reproducibility
- Easier validation
- Greater user trust

---

# Consequences

All analytical services developed for APL shall follow this model.

Future AI modules shall not introduce alternative response structures unless approved through a dedicated ADR.

---

# Exceptions

No exceptions are currently permitted.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Alternative 1

Return recommendations only.

Rejected because recommendations without context cannot be independently evaluated.

---

## Alternative 2

Merge observations and interpretations.

Rejected because objective facts and technical reasoning must remain distinguishable.

---

## Alternative 3

Adopt a three-level analytical model.

Accepted because it ensures transparency, explainability and scientific consistency.
