# ADR-003 – Separation of Raw Data and Derived Data

**Project:** Archery Performance Lab (APL)

**ADR ID:** ADR-003

**Status:** Accepted

**Date:** 2026-07-29

**Decision Makers:** Archery Performance Lab Team

---

# Context

APL is designed as a scientific platform for archery performance analysis.

The platform stores both measured data collected during training or competition and analytical results generated by formulas, statistical models or artificial intelligence.

Without a clear separation between measured and calculated information, future improvements to analytical models could alter historical records and compromise the reproducibility of analyses.

A permanent distinction between original observations and derived information is therefore required.

---

# Decision

APL adopts a strict separation between **Raw Data** and **Derived Data**.

Raw Data represents information that has been directly observed or measured.

Derived Data represents information calculated or inferred from Raw Data.

Raw Data shall never be modified after validation.

Derived Data may be regenerated whenever analytical models evolve.

---

# Definitions

## Raw Data

Raw Data consists of information obtained directly from measurements or observations.

Examples include:

- Score
- Timestamp
- Target Coordinates
- Environmental Conditions
- Equipment Configuration
- Arrow Configuration
- Shooting Distance
- Session Information

Raw Data is immutable.

---

## Derived Data

Derived Data consists of information produced through calculations or analytical processes.

Examples include:

- Average Score
- Group Diameter
- Vertical Dispersion
- Horizontal Dispersion
- Consistency Index
- Arrow Speed Estimation
- FOC Calculation
- Performance Indicators
- AI Recommendations

Derived Data depends on Raw Data.

---

# Architecture

```text
Measured Information
        │
        ▼
     Raw Data
        │
        ▼
 Validation (ACE)
        │
        ▼
 Analytical Models
        │
        ▼
   Derived Data
        │
        ▼
 Recommendations
```

Only validated Raw Data may be used for analysis.

---

# Rationale

This separation provides:

- Scientific integrity
- Historical preservation
- Analytical reproducibility
- Future-proof architecture
- Independent evolution of analytical models
- Full auditability

Historical measurements remain unchanged even if future algorithms improve.

---

# Consequences

The following principles apply:

- Raw Data is immutable.
- Derived Data may be regenerated.
- Historical analyses remain reproducible.
- Previous analytical results may coexist with newer versions.
- Every Derived Data record shall reference the analytical model that generated it.

---

# Versioning

Every Derived Data record shall include:

- Analysis Version
- Algorithm Version
- Calculation Date
- Confidence Level (if applicable)

This allows comparisons between different analytical generations.

---

# Relationship with AI

AIE shall never modify Raw Data.

AIE may generate:

- New Derived Data
- Updated Indicators
- Alternative Interpretations
- New Recommendations

Every generated result must remain traceable to its originating Raw Data.

---

# Validation

ACE validates Raw Data before storage.

Only validated Raw Data may enter analytical workflows.

Invalid or incomplete data shall not generate Derived Data.

---

# Exceptions

No exceptions are currently permitted.

Any future exception requires approval through a dedicated ADR.

---

# Alternatives Considered

## Alternative 1

Store only calculated results.

Rejected because original measurements would be lost.

---

## Alternative 2

Overwrite analytical results after every recalculation.

Rejected because historical analyses would become unreproducible.

---

## Alternative 3

Store Raw Data and Derived Data separately.

Accepted because it guarantees scientific consistency and long-term maintainability.
